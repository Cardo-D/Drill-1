Beginners guide to Pickleball🟢

Rules
  • Pickleball is played with a paddle and a plastic ball with holes.
  • The game can be played in singles or doubles.
  • The objective is to score points by hitting the ball into the opponent's court.
Equipment
  • Pickleball paddle
  • Pickleball ball
  • Pickleball court
Techniques
  • Serve
  • Volley
  • Drop shot
